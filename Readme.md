# Sajag Kishan

**Team:** CSITABMC

**Sajag Kishan** ("alert farmer") is an app that tells Nepali farmers what to grow and when, and tells agriculture officers where crops, land and fertilizer need attention.

## For Farmers

- Crop calendar tailored to their altitude and climate
- Weather alerts on their phone
- Disease alerts on their phone

## For Officers

- Map of what is grown where
- Visibility into which land is fallow or fertilized
- Guidance on where to send subsidy and fertilizer
